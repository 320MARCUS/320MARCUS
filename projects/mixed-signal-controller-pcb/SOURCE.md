## Scope of the public edition

The following changes were made in the isolated public working copy:

- Replaced the legacy competition title block with a clean English portfolio profile.
- Applied small silkscreen corrections without changing the circuit or principal routing.
- Exported the design and manufacturing files.
- Reordered the exported PDF pages by logical page identifier.
- Removed references that would expose personal identifiers or third-party examination material.

No deliberate change was made to:

- component selection or electrical topology;
- board outline, stackup, or copper-layer count;
- placement of major devices;
- routed copper geometry;
- library ownership.

## Attribution boundary

The historical board and its retained component choices are presented as an author-maintained engineering case. The portfolio package claims only the work that can be supported by the source project and the evidence in this directory: historical design retention, public-copy isolation, sanitization, title-block replacement, silk cleanup, export preparation, and static verification.

Third-party symbols, footprints, vendor reference circuits, and manufacturer names remain subject to their original terms. They are not relicensed by this portfolio package.
